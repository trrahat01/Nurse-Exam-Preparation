# Release Notes — v1.6.0

## What's New
- 🔥 **Daily Streak** — track your study streak and keep your momentum going
- 🏆 **Best Streak** — see your personal best on your profile
- ✅ **Today's Quiz** status — know instantly if you've practiced today
- 🔔 **Daily Reminder toggle** (Settings) — turn the 8 PM nudge on/off
- 🔔 **Streak reminder push** — "Day 🔥N — don't lose your streak!" keeps you coming back
- 🎨 Animated splash + cleaner startup (no blue screen / loading flash)
- 🔐 **Firebase Authentication** — sign up, sign in, email verification & password reset
- ☁️ **Supabase database** — live question bank, saved results & bookmarks
- 📊 **Firebase Analytics** — usage & active-user insights
- 🎯 **More AdMob placements** — banner, interstitial & native ads (free app)
- 🐛 Stability and performance fixes

## Technical
- Auth: Firebase `@react-native-firebase` (native) + AsyncStorage session persistence
- Database: Supabase (@supabase/supabase-js)
- Ads: `react-native-google-mobile-ads@15.8.3` — banner / interstitial / native + test-ads mode
- Analytics: `@react-native-firebase/analytics` (app_open / DAU)
