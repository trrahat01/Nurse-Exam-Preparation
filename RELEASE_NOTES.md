# Release Notes — v1.8.0

## What's New
- 🎯 **NCLEX-style Readiness Score** — a new "Predicted Exam Readiness" gauge on Home & Analytics estimates when you're ready to sit the exam
- 🏆 **Levels, XP & Badges** — earn XP for every correct answer, level up, and unlock badges (First Steps, On Fire, Pharmacology Pro, Sharp Shooter and more)
- 📊 **Category Leaderboard** — see your strongest subjects ranked in Analytics and Level & Badges
- 🔥 **Smarter streak reminders** — your daily push now says "Day N — take your 20 questions"
- ✅ **Weak Topics mastery** — correctly re-answering a missed question in practice automatically clears it from your Weak Topics list
- 🏅 **Badge celebrations** — unlock notifications right on your exam result screen
- 📣 **Production AdMob** — real ads enabled (test-ad fallback disabled) across home, practice, mock, category & results
