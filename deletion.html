How to delete your account and data

We respect your privacy. To permanently delete your account and all associated personal data from Nurse Exam Preparation, follow these steps.

  1. Send a deletion request email to our support team.
  2. Include the email address you registered your account with.
  3. We verify your request and permanently delete all your data within 30 days.

What data is deleted?

We permanently remove:

  • Your account credentials (email and password)
  • Your profile information (name)
  • Your exam results and progress history
