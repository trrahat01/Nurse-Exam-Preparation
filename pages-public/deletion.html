Data Deletion

Nurse Exam Preparation

Good news: your data is yours

Nurse Exam Preparation does not have accounts. The app is guest-only, so there is no account on our servers to delete and no email, password, name, or personal information is stored by us.

All of your study progress — exam results, bookmarks, wrong-answer records, streaks, XP, badges, and settings — is stored only on your own device.

How to delete your data

You can delete all of your app data at any time using either of these methods:

  1. Uninstall the app from your device, then clear the app's data via your device settings (Android: Settings → Apps → Nurse Exam Preparation → Clear data / Storage).
  2. Inside the app: Settings → Reset Seen Questions to clear question history. For a full local reset, reinstall the app.
Note: Because nothing is stored on our servers, uninstalling (with app-data cleared) permanently removes all of your on-device progress. There is nothing left for us to delete remotely.
