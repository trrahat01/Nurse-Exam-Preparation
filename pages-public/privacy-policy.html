Privacy Policy

Nurse Exam Preparation

Last updated: September 21, 2026

1. No Account — No Registration

Nurse Exam Preparation is a fully guest-only application. It does not offer accounts, registration, or sign-in, and it does not collect your email address, password, name, phone number, or any other personal identification data.

2. Your Study Data Stays on Your Device

  • Practice & Mock Exam Results
  • Question history / exposures
  • Bookmarked questions
  • Wrong-answer records
  • Daily practice and study streak statistics
  • Performance analytics & gamification (XP, levels, badges)
  • App settings (theme, notifications, reading preferences)

All of the above are stored locally on your device only. They are never uploaded, synced, or transmitted to us or to any server.

3. Anonymous Usage Analytics

The app uses Firebase Analytics to count anonymous events such as "app opened" and "screen viewed". These events do not contain your identity, your study data, or any personal information. They are used only to measure aggregate app usage.

4. Advertising

The app displays advertising powered by Google AdMob and Start.io. Advertising partners may use an advertising identifier and/or device-related identifiers to serve and report ads. This happens only within the ad services' own systems in accordance with their privacy policies and our Google Play Data Safety declarations. We do not sell or share any personal information.

5. Content Served From Our Database

Practice questions and study content are served to the app from our database (Supabase). The app only reads this public content; it never uploads your data to our database.

6. We Do Not Sell Your Information

We do not collect personal information, and we do not sell personal information to anyone.

7. Children's Privacy

The app is an educational study tool. It does not require or collect personal information from any user, including children. Because we hold no account or personal data, there is no personal information about children to disclose.

8. Data Deletion

Because all study progress is stored only on your device, you can delete it at any time by:

  • Uninstalling the app, or
  • Clearing the app's data (Settings > Reset Seen Questions, or your device's app-data clear option).

See our Data Deletion page for details.

9. Contact Us

For any privacy questions, email: mtcorestudio@gmail.com
