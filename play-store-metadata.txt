Nurse Exam Preparation — independent study tool for nursing candidates preparing for competitive exams.
Building on official nursing curriculum and published exam syllabi, the app offers 40,000+ practice questions across Nursing Science, Bangla, English, General Knowledge, and Math, with 60+ subcategories and full mock tests.

FEATURES:
- 40,000+ Practice Questions
- 5 Major Categories: Nursing Science, Bangla, English, General Knowledge, Math
- 60+ Subcategories covering nursing specialties
- Full Mock Tests with timers & auto-submit
- Instant Results & Answer Review with explanations
- 🔥 Daily Study Streak with daily reminders
- 📉 Weak Topics re-quiz until you master missed questions
- 🃏 Bookmarks-as-Flashcards review mode
- 🎯 NCLEX-style predicted readiness score
- 🏆 XP levels, badges & category leaderboards
- Performance Analytics & Progress Tracking
- Bookmark Questions for review
- Works Offline & Guest Mode

EXAMS PREPARED FOR:
- Government Nursing Recruitment Exams (see official sources below)
- Nursing Council & Board Exams
- BSc Nursing & Diploma in Nursing Exams
- Hospital Recruitment Exams
- Midwifery & Public Health Nursing Exams
- International Nursing Exams (NCLEX-style, HAAD-style, etc.)
